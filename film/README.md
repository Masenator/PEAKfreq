# PEAKfreq brand film

A 15-second, 1080p60 motion piece built entirely from the brand system: the logo fade, the
five frequencies, the real product packaging art, the summit scene and the wordmark. No stock
footage and no samples. Every frame and every sound is generated, so the film re-renders
exactly and updates when the brand does.

## Structure

| Time | Scene | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Signal | A flatline sweeps into a heartbeat. "Your body runs on rhythm." |
| 2–5.6s | Five frequencies | One line plays each rhythm (Cadence, Cellular, Circadian, Neural, Cardiac), each with its product and evidence grade |
| 5.4–6.5s | Dawn | The five waves separate and settle into the mountain ridgelines, then light bursts through |
| 6.3–9.6s | Ascent | The runner climbs, leaving the orange track, with live altitude, heart rate and cadence. "Every rhythm. One place." |
| 9.6–12s | Peak | The leap, lotus, orange, sun rays, a shockwave in the five colours, heart rate settling to "Peak frequency" |
| 12–15s | Resolve | Logo mark draws, PEAK stamps in, freq writes on, the pulse beats. "Reach your peak frequency. Realise your full potential." |

The sound design follows the picture. Monitor blips on the opening heartbeat, a kick and a
rising pentatonic note per frequency, a pulse locked to the runner's footsteps, a quickening
heartbeat into the leap, a sub drop and shimmer chord on the burst, and the monitor blip
returning on the logo's pulse. It's mastered to about -14 LUFS for social platforms.

## Render

```bash
pip install numpy scipy imageio-ffmpeg
npm i --no-save playwright && npx playwright install chromium
node film/render.mjs              # film/out/peakfreq-brand-film.mp4, ~12 min on 4 cores
node film/render.mjs --preview    # every 4th frame at 15fps, ~4 min
```

To refresh the packaging art and brand shapes after a site change, run the site, then
`node film/extract-assets.mjs` (set `SITE_URL` if it isn't on port 3000).

To scrub the film in a browser, serve this folder and open `index.html?t=11.3`, or call
`render(seconds)` in the console.

## Files

- `index.html`, `reel.js`: the film. `render(t)` draws any instant deterministically.
- `synth.py`: the soundtrack, synthesised with numpy and scipy.
- `assets.json`: product art, summit layers, wordmark and mark, generated by `extract-assets.mjs`.
- `fonts/`: Archivo, Inter and JetBrains Mono, all SIL Open Font License 1.1.
